Frequently Asked Questions

uPVC products do not warp, rot, or get damaged by termites. They require only basic wiping to maintain good condition, compared to the painting and varnishing required for wooden windows and doors. uPVC does not get heated or cooled easily, making it a superior choice for energy efficiency. uPVC windows and doors offer a leakproof design that prevents rainwater from entering your home. When closed, uPVC windows and doors significantly reduce noise, providing a peaceful home environment.

Yes. uPVC windows can easily replace your old wooden or other windows since they offer extensive customization options to match the size, style, color, and other features such as mesh and grill for your windows.

uPVC windows and doors are reinforced with strong galvanized steel reinforcements that add to their strength and durability. Our uPVC windows and doors are fitted with multi-point locking mechanisms and high-quality locks among other hardware to offer superior security and safety.

The price of uPVC windows and doors depends on various factors such as the type of uPVC profile, type of windows and doors, glass, color, and hardware. The price of uPVC windows and doors starts at Rs. 350/sqft.
